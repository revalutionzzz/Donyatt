EA warning page for the River Isle

How this works: the status comes from the Environment Agency's Donyatt river gauge, upstream rainfall at Chard and EA flood warnings. Avoid means the river is at or near the level where roads have flooded before (1.80 m), or the EA has a flood warning in force. Caution means the river is above normal, rising fast, or there's heavy rain upstream. Open only means the data shows no flooding. Conditions can change fast, so always follow road signs and the police.
