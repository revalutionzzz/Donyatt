How the outlook works

A statistical model trained on the Environment Agency's records for Donyatt and the Chard rain gauge since 2017. It looks at how high the river is, how fast it's changing, how much rain has fallen over the last 3 days and, when available, the rain forecast. Tested on floods from 2023 to 2026 that it never saw during training, it gave warning before all of them, typically about 2½ hours before roads flooded. An Elevated or High outlook puts the A358 on Caution. It can't see everything (blocked drains, other streams), so always follow road signs and never drive into floodwater.

Past floods at Donyatt

Highest level each year from the EA's records. Bars past the red line are years when roads flooded.

How this works. The status comes from the Environment Agency's Donyatt river gauge, upstream rainfall at Chard and EA flood warnings. Avoid means the river is at or close to the level where the A358 has flooded before (1.80 m), or the EA has a flood warning in force. Caution means the river is above normal, rising fast, or there's heavy rain upstream. Open only means the data shows no flooding. The river can rise from normal to road-flooding level in under an hour, so always follow road signs and the police.

Driver reports and privacy. When you report a road, we keep the road, your choice, the time and the river readings at that moment, so we can learn when each road really floods. We don't store names, locations or IP addresses. To stop spam we use Cloudflare Turnstile and an anonymous code that changes every day, so reports can't be linked to you over time. Photos have their location and camera details removed, stay hidden until checked or confirmed by another driver, and are deleted after 2 days.
